College Terminology

Semester
A half-year academic term, usually about 16 to 18 weeks long.
Credit
A unit that measures the workload and value of a course.
CGPA
Cumulative Grade Point Average, the overall average of grades across all semesters.
Syllabus
A document outlining the topics and schedule of a course.
Elective
A course chosen by the student based on personal interest rather than being compulsory.
