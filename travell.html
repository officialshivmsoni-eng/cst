Famous Places in India

Home | Destinations | Foods | Gallery | Contact

1. Taj Mahal, Agra

The Taj Mahal is a magnificent white marble monument and a UNESCO World Heritage Site built by Emperor Shah Jahan.

2. Red Fort, Delhi

The Red Fort is a historic fort known for its red sandstone walls and Mughal architecture.

3. Jaipur, Rajasthan

Jaipur, the Pink City, is famous for its royal palaces, forts and colourful markets.

4. Kerala Backwaters

Kerala is known for its peaceful backwaters, green landscapes and traditional houseboats.

5. Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh

Varanasi is an ancient city on the River Ganges, famous for its ghats and spiritual traditions.
